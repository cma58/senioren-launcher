# Privacy Policy — Senioren Launcher (v1.0.0)

**🌐 Language:** **English** · [Русский](PRIVACY_RU.md) · [Nederlands](PRIVACY.md)

## Summary
Senioren Launcher is designed with a "Privacy by Design" approach. We collect **no** personal data whatsoever on external servers.

- ❌ **No analytics**: we do not track how you use the app.
- ❌ **No advertising**: the app contains no ads or trackers.
- ❌ **No cloud storage**: all your contacts, messages and settings stay locally on your device.
- ❌ **No data sales**: data is never shared with commercial parties.
- ✅ **Open Source**: the source code is fully transparent and verifiable.

## Data Minimisation
In the context of the cooperation with local governments (such as the city of **Ghent**) and care organisations, we apply strict data minimisation. Only the permissions that are necessary for the functionality of the launcher (such as SOS, SMS management and calling) are requested.

## External Communication (SMS)
The app only responds to SMS commands from trusted contacts (caregivers) that you have set up. This communication runs directly over your provider's mobile network, without any involvement of our servers.

## Language Setting & Internet Use
The chosen interface language is stored only on the device. The app makes only these internet requests, and none of them contains contacts, messages, medication data or other personal content:
- **Weather** (Open-Meteo): the coordinates or the place name you entered, and the language, to receive the forecast.
- **App updates** (GitHub): a check for a new release.
- **Internet radio**: the audio stream of the station you play and, if you search, the search text (radio-browser.info).

## GDPR Compliance
Because no personal data is processed outside the device, the app fully complies with the GDPR guidelines for local data processing. Users have full control and can erase all data by uninstalling the app or by clearing the app's data in the Android settings.
